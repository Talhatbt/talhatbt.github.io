Privacy Policy

What Tillo collects, where your shop's data lives, and how to remove it.

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy applies to the Tillo point-of-sale application for Android ("Tillo", "we", "us", "our"), published on Google Play by TSolutions DWC LLC under the package name com.tsolution.smartpos. Tillo lets a shop sell, take payments, print or share receipts and keep track of products and stock, all from a phone.

By using Tillo you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the application.

In short. Tillo has no sign-up and no account. A shop set up on the phone is kept only on that phone: your products, prices, stock, sales and staff are never sent to us. There is no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting in the app. The only things that leave your phone are what Google Play and RevenueCat need to sell and verify a Tillo Pro subscription, and the limited diagnostic data Google's on-device barcode scanner reports. We do not sell your data.

1Information we collect

Your shop's data — kept on your phone

When you set up Tillo as a new shop, everything you enter is stored in a private database inside the app's own storage on your phone:

  • Your shop's details: its name, phone number, address, currency, tax settings, and the receipt header and footer you write.
  • Products: names, prices, cost prices, barcodes, categories, options such as sizes and colours, and stock levels and stock movements.
  • Sales: the items sold, totals, tax, discounts, how each sale was paid (cash, card or wallet — recorded only; Tillo does not process card or wallet payments), and invoice numbers.
  • Staff and shifts, if you use them: staff names, and sign-in PINs stored only as salted, one-way hashes. Shift records hold opening floats and cash-up counts.

We never receive this data, cannot see it, and hold no copy of it. Tillo has no server for a phone-owned shop. The app also opts out of Android's automatic cloud backup, so the system does not copy it anywhere either — the only copies are the backup files you choose to make (section 4).

Subscription information

Tillo is free to use, with an optional Tillo Pro subscription. Purchases are made through Google Play and verified through RevenueCat. RevenueCat assigns your installation a random, anonymous identifier — it is not linked to your name, email or shop — and receives the product you bought, its purchase and renewal status, and basic device information such as the app version, operating system and your IP address. We never receive your card or bank details; those stay with Google.

Your plan is remembered on the phone, so Tillo keeps working when it is offline.

Barcode scanner diagnostics

The camera scanner uses Google's ML Kit barcode scanning, with its model bundled inside the app, so codes are read entirely on your phone. Camera images are never stored and never sent to us or to Google. ML Kit may send Google limited technical data about how the scanner is performing — such as device model, operating system, app version and scan performance metrics — under Google's ML Kit terms. It does not include the images or the barcodes read.

If you use an activation code from a business

Instead of setting up a new shop, a business that uses the SmartPOS back office can give you an activation code to connect the phone to its account. In that case the phone exchanges data with the SmartPOS cloud service we operate for that business: it receives the business's products, settings and staff, and sends it the sales made on the phone, which staff member made them, and the shift records. That data is held on behalf of the business that issued the code, which decides how it is used and is your first point of contact about it. A phone set up as its own shop never contacts this service.

2What we do not collect

  • No account, email address, phone number or password is ever asked for.
  • No advertising and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics, usage tracking or crash reporting.
  • No location. Bluetooth is used only to connect to printers you have already paired, so the app never scans for nearby devices.
  • No contacts, photos, files, microphone or SMS. Backup files are read and written only through the Android file picker, one file at a time, when you choose.
  • No personal information about your customers. Tillo does not ask for a customer's name, phone number or email to make a sale.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade any information.

4Backups, receipts and printing

These features move your data only where you send it:

  • Backup. Tillo can save your whole shop to a single file, in a place you choose with the Android file picker — your phone's storage, Google Drive, or any other provider you have installed. The file is not encrypted, so that a forgotten password can never make your only copy unrecoverable; it holds your shop's details, products, stock and sales, and staff PINs only as hashes. Treat it like any business record and keep it somewhere private. Restoring reads a file you pick in the same way. We never receive a copy.
  • Sharing a receipt. When you share a receipt, Tillo hands its text to the Android share sheet and the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or email) sends it. That app's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Printing. Receipts are sent directly over Bluetooth to your printer. The printer's name, address and paper width are remembered on the phone only.

6How long data is kept, and how to delete it

  • Your shop's data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can edit or remove products at any time. Uninstalling Tillo, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, permanently erases everything — we cannot recover it, so make a backup first if you want to keep it. Because we never hold a copy, there is nothing you need to ask us to delete.
  • Backup files stay wherever you saved them until you delete them there.
  • Subscription records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat for as long as needed to honour and restore your purchase and to meet tax and accounting obligations. You can manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.
  • Data held for a business (activation-code phones only) is kept for as long as that business keeps its SmartPOS account. Ask the business, or contact us, to have it removed.

7Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal data about you. For data on your phone, you can exercise all of these directly in the app. For anything else described here, contact us at the address below and we will respond within a reasonable time. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

8Security

Your shop's data is held in Tillo's private storage area, which Android keeps separate from other apps. Staff PINs are stored only as salted hashes, never as the PINs themselves. Everything Tillo sends over the internet is encrypted with TLS.

Because your shop lives on your phone, the phone is what protects it. Use a screen lock, and keep regular backups somewhere safe — if the phone is lost or broken, a backup file is the only way to get your shop back.

9Children

Tillo is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
