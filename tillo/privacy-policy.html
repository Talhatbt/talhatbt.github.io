Privacy Policy

What Tillo collects, where your shop's data lives, and how to remove it.

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy applies to the Tillo point-of-sale application for Android ("Tillo", "we", "us", "our"), published on Google Play by TSolutions DWC LLC under the package name com.tsolution.smartpos. Tillo lets a shop sell, take payments, print or share receipts and keep track of products and stock, all from a phone. We also run the SmartPOS back office, a cloud service for businesses with several tills or stores. The version of Tillo on Google Play cannot connect to it; section 1 explains what applies to a business that joins it by arrangement with us.

By using Tillo you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the application.

In short. Tillo has no sign-up and no account. A shop set up on the phone is kept only on that phone: your products, prices, stock, sales and staff are never sent to us. There is no advertising in the app. What leaves your phone is what Google Play and RevenueCat need to sell and verify a Tillo Pro subscription, the limited diagnostic data Google's on-device barcode scanner reports, your shop's country and the app version when Tillo checks for updated app settings (including the twice-a-day check behind update notifications, which come from the app itself, not from a push service), and anonymous usage data and crash reports that help us improve Tillo — which you can switch off in Settings › Share usage data. Usage data never includes your products, prices, sales or customers. Customer details you record for credit, and product photos, stay on your phone. When you email us from Settings › Help, your email app sends the message, not Tillo. The version on Google Play has no way to send your shop's data anywhere. We do not sell your data.

1Information we collect

Your shop's data — kept on your phone

When you set up Tillo as a new shop, everything you enter is stored in a private database inside the app's own storage on your phone:

  • Your shop's details: its name, phone number, address, currency, tax settings, and the receipt header and footer you write.
  • Products: names, prices, cost prices, barcodes, categories, options such as sizes and colours, and stock levels and stock movements.
  • Product photos, if you add them: a small copy of a picture you take with the camera or choose from your gallery, kept inside the app. Tillo keeps only that copy and never reads the rest of your photos.
  • Sales: the items sold, totals, tax, discounts, how each sale was paid (cash, card, wallet or on credit, or several of these on one sale — recorded only; Tillo does not process card or wallet payments), and invoice numbers.
  • Customers, if you sell on credit: the name, phone number and note you enter for each customer, and their credit history — what they bought on credit, what they paid back, and any corrections you made. Tillo does not ask for anything else about a customer, and you can make a sale without one.
  • Staff and shifts, if you use them: staff names, and sign-in PINs stored only as salted, one-way hashes. Shift records hold opening floats and cash-up counts.

We never receive this data, cannot see it, and hold no copy of it. Tillo has no server for a phone-owned shop. The app also opts out of Android's automatic cloud backup, so the system does not copy it anywhere either — the only copies are the backup files you choose to make (section 4).

Your customers' details are yours to look after. When you record a customer, you are the one collecting their information, and you decide what to keep. Only record what you need to keep track of credit, tell customers if the law where you trade requires it, and if a customer asks, edit their record to clear their phone number and note once they no longer owe you anything.

App settings and updates

When Tillo starts, every few hours while it stays open, and about twice a day in the background when your phone has a connection, it asks our server for the current app settings: whether a newer version is available, any short message we have for Tillo users (such as a support holiday), the Free plan's product limit, the support contact details, and switches that can turn a feature off if it is broken. The request contains only your shop's country (so the settings can differ by country) and the app's version number. It carries no identifier, no shop name and nothing about your products, sales or customers. Our hosting provider, Supabase, sees your IP address as part of the connection, as any web server does. If the server cannot be reached, Tillo keeps the last settings it received.

Update notifications. If the background check finds a new version or a new message, Tillo shows one notification on your phone, once for each. These notifications are created by the app on your phone: Tillo uses no push-notification service, so no push token or device address is created or sent anywhere. Tillo asks for permission to show notifications after a sale, not when you first open it, and if you decline it does not ask again for 30 days. You can turn them off at any time in Android Settings › Apps › Tillo › Notifications. Which version or message was last shown is remembered on the phone only.

Updating from inside the app. When you tap Update, Tillo asks Google Play, through Google's in-app update service on your phone, to download the new version while you keep working, and then offers Restart to finish. This happens between Tillo and the Play Store app on your phone, under Google's terms; we receive nothing from it. If Google Play cannot do this, Tillo opens its Google Play listing instead.

When you contact us

Settings › Help can open your own email app with a message already written to us. The backoffice request form does the same, if it is offered where you are. Tillo itself sends nothing. You see the whole message and decide whether to send it. If you do, we receive:

  • your email address, from your email app;
  • what the message says: your shop's name, its country, your plan, the app version and a reference code. The reference code is made from the random identifier this installation generated (see "Usage data" below), so we can match your message to your app. Clear this phone changes it;
  • for a backoffice request, also what you typed into the form: your name, phone number, email if you gave one, the number of branches and phones, what you need, how to reply, and any note.

We use these messages only to reply to you and to deal with your request. We keep them in our email for as long as we need to help you, and we delete them on request. Your email provider's privacy policy also applies to the message.

Subscription information

Tillo is free to use, with an optional Tillo Pro subscription. Purchases are made through Google Play and verified through RevenueCat. RevenueCat assigns your installation a random, anonymous identifier — it is not linked to your name, email or shop — and receives the product you bought, its purchase and renewal status, and basic device information such as the app version, operating system and your IP address. We never receive your card or bank details; those stay with Google.

Your plan is remembered on the phone, so Tillo keeps working when it is offline.

Usage data and crash reports — optional

Tillo uses PostHog to understand how the app is used and where it goes wrong, so we can fix and improve it. This is on by default and can be switched off at any time in Settings › Share usage data; once off, nothing more is sent.

  • What is sent: which screens are opened and which actions are taken — for example "a product was added", "a sale was completed by cash with 3 items", "the Pro sheet was opened from Reports" — with counts, yes/no answers and categories; the app version, plan (Free or Pro), your shop's country and currency, and basic device information such as the operating system, device model and your IP address; and, if the app crashes, a technical report of where in the app's code it failed.
  • Session recordings: a recording of how the screens are used — taps and the order of screens — so we can see where people get stuck. Every screen that shows your products, prices, stock, sales or shop details is blacked out in the recording, as are all text fields and images; only setup, help and empty screens are visible.
  • What is never sent: your shop's name, phone number or address; product names, barcodes, prices or stock; the amount of any sale (at most a broad band such as "100–999"); staff names; or anything about your customers.
  • Identifier: the data is tied to a random identifier that the app generates for this installation — not your name, email, device hardware ID or advertising ID. Clear this phone replaces it with a new one.

Barcode scanner diagnostics

The camera scanner uses Google's ML Kit barcode scanning, with its model bundled inside the app, so codes are read entirely on your phone. Camera images are never stored and never sent to us or to Google. ML Kit may send Google limited technical data about how the scanner is performing — such as device model, operating system, app version and scan performance metrics — under Google's ML Kit terms. It does not include the images or the barcodes read.

Businesses on the SmartPOS back office (by arrangement only)

The version of Tillo on Google Play cannot connect to the SmartPOS back office: it has no activation option and never sends your shop's data to us. A business that wants the back office contacts us, and we set up its tills with it directly. For those tills only:

  • Each till is linked to the business's account with a single-use activation code created by the business's administrator. A code expires after 24 hours and stops working once used.
  • Sent from the till: each sale made on it — the items, prices, tax, discounts, totals, how it was paid, the invoice number, the time, and which staff member made it — and each shift, with its opening float and cash-up count. When the code is entered, the till also sends a random identifier that the app generates for this installation. It is not the device's hardware ID or advertising ID.
  • Received by the till: the business's store details, products, prices and categories, its staff members' names and roles with their PINs as one-way hashes, and its till settings, colours and logo.
  • The till keeps working offline and sends sales when it next has a connection. The server gives the till a private access token, and stores only a one-way hash of it.

All of this data belongs to the business. That business decides how the data is used and is your first point of contact about it; we process it on the business's behalf. Clear this phone in Settings removes the business's data from the till, and will not run while any sale is still waiting to be sent, so none is lost. Data already sent stays in the business's back office (see section 6).

If your business uses the SmartPOS back office

The back office is a separate website used by the owners and managers of those businesses; people who use Tillo from Google Play never need it. Each back-office user signs in with an email address and password, handled by our hosting provider's authentication service. Passwords are stored only in hashed form. In the back office the business enters its organization and store details, its products and prices, its staff names and roles, its till settings, and optionally a logo and brand colours. Uploaded logos are stored at a public web address so that tills can display them without signing in, so don't upload anything you would not put on a receipt. The back office has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

2What we do not collect

  • No account, email address or password is ever asked for in the app. The backoffice request form asks for a name and a phone number, and optionally an email address, but they reach us only if you send the email yourself (see "When you contact us"). Only a business's back-office users sign in, on the back-office website.
  • No advertising and no advertising ID.
  • No push notifications from a server and no push token. Update notifications are made by the app on your phone (section 1).
  • No usage data that identifies you or your shop — see "Usage data and crash reports" above, and switch it off whenever you like.
  • No location. Bluetooth is used only to connect to printers you have already paired, so the app never scans for nearby devices.
  • No access to your contacts, photo library, files, microphone or SMS. A product photo comes from the camera, or from the Android photo picker, which shares only the one picture you choose. Backup files are read and written only through the Android file picker, one file at a time, when you choose.
  • Nothing about your customers reaches us. The customer details you record for credit stay on your phone and in your backups (section 1). A sale never needs a customer.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade any information.

4Backups, receipts and printing

These features move your data only where you send it:

  • Backup. Tillo can save your whole shop to a single file, in a place you choose with the Android file picker — your phone's storage, Google Drive, or any other provider you have installed. The file is not encrypted, so that a forgotten password can never make your only copy unrecoverable; it holds your shop's details, products and their photos, stock, sales, and your customers with their names, phone numbers and credit history, with staff PINs only as hashes. Because it contains your customers' details, treat it like any business record and keep it somewhere private. Restoring reads a file you pick in the same way. We never receive a copy.
  • Sharing a receipt. When you share a receipt, Tillo hands its text to the Android share sheet and the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or email) sends it. That app's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Payment reminders. When you send a customer a reminder, Tillo opens WhatsApp with that customer's phone number and a message showing what they owe. You see the message, and WhatsApp sends it only if you tap send. WhatsApp's privacy policy then applies. A credit settlement slip is shared the same way as a receipt.
  • Printing. Receipts and shelf labels are sent directly over Bluetooth to your printer. A label carries the product's name, price and barcode; if a product has no barcode, Tillo gives it an in-store code, saved with the product on your phone, so its label scans at the till. The printer's name, address and paper width are remembered on the phone only.

6How long data is kept, and how to delete it

  • Your shop's data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can edit or remove products at any time. Uninstalling Tillo, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, permanently erases everything — we cannot recover it, so make a backup first if you want to keep it. Because we never hold a copy, there is nothing you need to ask us to delete.
  • Backup files stay wherever you saved them until you delete them there.
  • A customer's details stay on your phone with your sales records. You can edit a customer at any time to change their name or clear their phone number and note. Archiving a customer hides them from the list, but someone who still owes you stays visible, so a debt is never lost. Uninstalling Tillo or Clear this phone erases them, as it erases everything else. Their details also stay in any backup file that contains them.
  • Emails you send us are kept in our mailbox for as long as we need them to help you. Ask us and we will delete them.
  • Usage data and crash reports are kept in PostHog for a limited period and then deleted; session recordings are kept for a shorter period than events. Switching off Share usage data stops anything new being sent. Because this data is tied only to a random identifier, we cannot look it up by your name — to have it deleted, contact us and we will help you find your installation's identifier.
  • Subscription records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat for as long as needed to honour and restore your purchase and to meet tax and accounting obligations. You can manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.
  • Data held for a business (the SmartPOS back office and the tills set up for it) is kept for as long as that business keeps its SmartPOS account, because sales records are business and tax records. The business can ask us to delete its account and all of its data. If you are a staff member, ask the business first, or contact us. Clearing a till removes the data from that till only.
  • Back-office sign-in details are kept while the user has access to the business's account. To have a back-office login deleted, contact us at the address below.

7Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal data about you. For data on your phone, you can exercise all of these directly in the app. For anything else described here, contact us at the address below and we will respond within a reasonable time. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

8Security

Your shop's data is held in Tillo's private storage area, which Android keeps separate from other apps. Staff PINs are stored only as salted hashes, never as the PINs themselves. Everything Tillo sends over the internet is encrypted with TLS. For back-office businesses, each business's data in the SmartPOS cloud service is kept separate from every other business's by access rules enforced in the database. Activation codes are single-use and expire after 24 hours.

Because your shop lives on your phone, the phone is what protects it. Use a screen lock, and keep regular backups somewhere safe — if the phone is lost or broken, a backup file is the only way to get your shop back.

9Children

Tillo is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
