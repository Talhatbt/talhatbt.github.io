Delete Your Account

Speakline · TSolutions DWC LLC

You can permanently delete your Speakline account and the data associated with it at any time. Follow the steps below.

Option 1 — Delete from within the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Speakline app and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to Menu → Profile → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm your choice. The deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email info@tsolutions-dwc.com from the email address registered to your Speakline account with the subject line "Account deletion request". We will process your request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, email address, plan/tier information).
  • Your dictation history (all stored transcripts and their metadata).
  • Your trial and entitlement records.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Billing records — information required for accounting, tax, and fraud-prevention obligations may be retained for the period required by law (typically up to 7 years). These records do not include your dictation content.
  • Aggregated, anonymized analytics — usage statistics that cannot be linked back to you may be retained after deletion.
  • Data in backups — may take up to 30 additional days to be fully purged from backup systems after deletion.

Your other privacy controls

You don't need to delete your account to remove your data. You can delete individual dictations from your History at any time without deleting the account, or reset/moderate stored preferences in the app's settings. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
