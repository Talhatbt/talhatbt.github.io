Delete Your Data

Speakline · TSolutions DWC LLC

Speakline lets you delete your dictation data at any time — without deleting your account. Individual deletions take effect immediately.

Delete a single dictation (without deleting your account)

  1. Open the Speakline app and ensure you are signed in.
  2. Open History.
  3. Select the dictation you want to remove.
  4. Tap the Delete option and confirm.

Delete all of your dictations at once

  1. Open the Speakline app and ensure you are signed in.
  2. Go to Menu → Profile → Account.
  3. Choose Clear all history and confirm. Your account and preferences remain intact.

What gets deleted

  • The transcribed text and metadata for the selected dictation(s), including word count, duration, timestamp, language, and the name/package of the app you dictated into.

What is not affected

  • Deleting dictations does not delete your account, email, name, or subscription.
  • Cloud transcription audio is not stored by our provider and is not retrievable after transcription.
  • Aggregated, anonymized analytics (which contain no transcripts) may persist.

Retention on backups

After you delete data, residual copies in our backup systems may take up to 30 additional days to be fully purged.

To delete your entire account and all associated data instead, see our account deletion page.
